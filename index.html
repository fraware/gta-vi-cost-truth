01 / HARDWARE

Already own a PS5?

Count the game and your chosen extras. The confirmed single-player release has offline play on PlayStation, with no mandatory PS Plus subscription for the base story.

PlayStation listing ↗
02 / CLOUD

Xbox One has a route.

Microsoft confirms GTA VI streaming to Xbox One and Series X|S at launch, and lists prepaid playtime packs. The game purchase is separate. Other devices and advertising-supported access have their own eligibility limits.

Xbox announcement ↗
03 / PHYSICAL

A box doesn't contain a disc.

Rockstar states that physical copies carry a digital download code. Factor in your connection, local store rules, and code-region compatibility before buying.

Rockstar support ↗
04 / PLATFORM

No announced native PC edition.

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S are the confirmed native launch systems. Cloud streaming on a supported PC is a different proposition and requires verification of GTA VI availability on that device.
